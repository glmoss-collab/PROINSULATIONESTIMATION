2. HOW LARGE-DUCT SF WAS CALCULATED
   ======================================================================

   Rectangular (correct):  SF = 2 × (W + H + 2t) / 12 × LF
   Example 86" × 56" with 2" wrap:  2×(86+56+4)/12 = 24.33 SF per LF
   The estimator engine's old parser treated "86x56" as 86" ROUND
   (π×86/12 ≈ 22.5 SF/LF) and ignored the second dimension. Not used.
   Fittings: +15% SF on horizontal, +8% SF on vertical risers.
   Scale: 1/8" = 1'-0" on M2 sheets (1 PDF point = 1/9 foot).
